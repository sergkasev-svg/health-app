# 1. CBC / ОАК — CORE LOGIC

## Hemoglobin (Hb)
Meaning:
- reflects oxygen transport and anemia/hypoxia risk

Low Hb:
- anemia
- blood loss
- iron deficiency
- B12 / folate deficiency
- chronic disease context

High Hb:
- dehydration / hemoconcentration
- chronic hypoxia
- smoking
- less often erythrocytosis

How to explain:
- Низкий гемоглобин может соответствовать анемии или кровопотере.
- Повышенный гемоглобин часто бывает при обезвоживании или хронической нехватке кислорода.

Next checks:
- ferritin
- serum iron / TIBC if needed
- B12
- folate
- reticulocytes
- CRP / inflammation context

---

## RBC / Erythrocytes
Low RBC:
- anemia
- blood loss
- deficiency states
- marrow suppression

High RBC:
- dehydration
- hypoxia
- erythrocytosis

Important:
- interpret together with Hb, Hct, MCV, MCH, RDW

---

## Hematocrit (Hct)
Low Hct:
- anemia
- overhydration

High Hct:
- dehydration
- increased red cell mass

Patient explanation:
- Гематокрит помогает понять, не "сгущена" ли кровь и нет ли снижения доли эритроцитов.

---

## Color index / MCV / MCH / MCHC / RDW
Use for anemia patterning.

### Microcytic / hypochromic pattern
Usually:
- iron deficiency
- chronic blood loss
- less often thalassemia / chronic disease

### Macrocytic pattern
Usually:
- B12 deficiency
- folate deficiency
- liver disease
- alcohol
- some drugs

### High RDW
Often:
- mixed deficiency
- evolving iron deficiency
- recovery after treatment

Simple explanation:
- Эти показатели помогают понять, какого типа анемия вероятнее: железодефицитная, B12/фолат-дефицитная или смешанная.

Next checks:
- ferritin
- B12
- folate
- reticulocytes
- liver tests if macrocytosis

---

## Reticulocytes
Meaning:
- marrow response marker

High reticulocytes:
- recovery after blood loss / hemolysis / treatment response

Low reticulocytes:
- low marrow production
- deficiency states
- renal / endocrine / marrow problems

Important:
- if anemia + low reticulocytes -> think underproduction
- if anemia + high reticulocytes -> think blood loss / hemolysis / recovery

---

## Platelets (PLT)
Low platelets:
- infection
- immune thrombocytopenia
- marrow suppression
- hypersplenism
- deficiency context less commonly discussed as sole cause

High platelets:
- reactive inflammation
- iron deficiency
- recovery after bleeding
- dehydration
- less often myeloproliferative disease

Important:
- severe thrombocytopenia + bleeding signs = urgent review
- very high persistent platelets = doctor evaluation

---

## ESR / СОЭ
Meaning:
- nonspecific inflammation marker

High ESR:
- infection
- chronic inflammation
- autoimmune process
- anemia
- malignancy context
- tissue damage

Low ESR:
- less clinically useful alone
- may occur with polycythemia and some protein states

Rule:
- ESR alone never makes diagnosis
- interpret with CRP, symptoms, CBC, age

Patient explanation:
- СОЭ — неспецифический показатель. Он может повышаться при воспалении, инфекции, аутоиммунных процессах и анемии, но сам по себе не показывает точную причину.

# 2. LEUKOCYTE FORMULA

## WBC total
High WBC:
- infection
- inflammation
- stress response
- steroid effect
- hematologic disease if marked/persistent

Low WBC:
- viral infection
- marrow suppression
- drugs
- autoimmune causes
- severe deficiency context

Red flags:
- very low WBC + fever
- persistent leukopenia
- very high WBC without obvious infection

---

## Neutrophils
High neutrophils:
- bacterial infection
- acute inflammation
- steroids
- stress

Low neutrophils:
- viral infection
- drug effect
- marrow suppression
- autoimmune neutropenia
- benign childhood neutropenia in specific age groups possible

Red flag:
- neutropenia + fever = urgent medical evaluation

---

## Lymphocytes
High lymphocytes:
- viral infection
- recovery phase
- some chronic infections
- hematologic disease if persistent and marked

Low lymphocytes:
- stress
- steroid effect
- severe systemic illness
- immune deficiency context

Explain simply:
- Лимфоциты чаще растут при вирусных инфекциях, а нейтрофилы — чаще при бактериальных.

---

## Monocytes
High monocytes:
- recovery after infection
- chronic inflammation
- some viral/bacterial/parasitic infections

Low monocytes:
- usually less specific alone

---

## Eosinophils
High eosinophils:
- allergy / atopy
- parasitic infection
- drug reaction
- eosinophilic disorders

Low eosinophils:
- usually not informative alone
- may fall during acute stress / acute infection / steroid use

Patient explanation:
- Эозинофилы часто повышаются при аллергии или паразитарных инвазиях, но нужны жалобы и контекст.

---

## Basophils
High basophils:
- allergy context
- chronic inflammation
- rarely myeloproliferative disorders if persistent and marked

# 3. URINALYSIS / ОАМ — CORE LOGIC

## Protein in urine
Possible meaning:
- transient after fever/exercise
- kidney inflammation / glomerular issues
- dehydration

If persistent:
- repeat urinalysis
- urine protein quantification if needed
- kidney evaluation

## Leukocytes in urine
Possible meaning:
- urinary tract inflammation / infection
- contamination if poor collection
- vulvovaginal contamination in girls/women

Need:
- symptoms: pain, frequency, fever
- sometimes urine culture

## Nitrites / bacteria
Suggest:
- bacterial UTI more likely
But:
- interpret with symptoms and collection quality

## Erythrocytes / blood in urine
May mean:
- contamination (menstruation)
- stones
- infection
- kidney disease
- trauma

If persistent hematuria:
- doctor evaluation required

## Ketones
May appear with:
- fasting
- vomiting
- fever
- dehydration
- poor intake
- diabetes context

## Glucose in urine
Needs:
- blood glucose assessment
- diabetes exclusion if persistent

## Specific gravity
High:
- concentrated urine / dehydration
Low:
- dilute urine / excess fluids / renal concentrating issue if persistent

## pH
Helpful but nonspecific:
- diet
- infection with certain organisms
- metabolic context

Collection rule:
- Always ask how urine was collected.
- Bad collection can create false leukocytes/bacteria/mucus.

# 4. BASIC BIOCHEMISTRY / LIVER PANEL

## ALT / AST
Use:
- hepatocellular injury context
- muscle context for AST as well

High ALT / AST:
- viral hepatitis
- fatty liver
- drug-induced injury
- toxin/alcohol
- muscle injury (especially AST)
- intense exercise

Interpretation rule:
- isolated mild increase = repeat + context
- severe increase = urgent evaluation depending on symptoms

## De Ritis ratio (AST/ALT)
May help but never use alone.
Do not overstate it.

## Bilirubin
High total/direct/indirect:
- hemolysis
- Gilbert syndrome
- liver disease
- cholestasis / bile obstruction

Rule:
- use with ALT, AST, ALP, GGT, symptoms, stool/urine color

## ALP / GGT
ALP high:
- cholestasis
- bone growth in children
- bone disease

GGT high:
- supports hepatobiliary source
- drug/alcohol/enzyme induction context

Important:
- in children ALP may be physiologically higher because of bone growth

## Total protein / albumin
Low:
- malnutrition
- protein loss
- liver synthesis issues
- inflammation / chronic disease context

High total protein:
- dehydration
- chronic inflammation
- paraproteinemia context

# 5. DEFICIENCY PATTERNS TO FLAG

## Possible iron deficiency pattern
Typical combination:
- Hb low or low-normal
- MCV low / MCH low
- RDW high
- platelets may be reactive-high
Then suggest:
- ferritin
- iron studies
- blood loss history
- diet review

## Possible B12 / folate deficiency pattern
Typical combination:
- macrocytosis
- low Hb or mixed anemia
- neurologic or mucosal symptoms possible
Then suggest:
- B12
- folate
- homocysteine
- sometimes methylmalonic acid if available

## Possible inflammation / infection pattern
Typical combination:
- WBC or neutrophils high
- ESR high
- symptoms present
Then suggest:
- CRP
- focused infection evaluation
- repeat CBC if needed

## Possible allergy / parasite pattern
Typical combination:
- eosinophils high
- allergy symptoms / rash / itching / GI complaints
Then suggest:
- allergy history
- stool / parasite workup only when clinically justified

# 6. TRIAGE / ESCALATION RULES

## Urgent doctor / emergency assessment
If any of the following:
- severe weakness + very low Hb suspected
- active bleeding
- platelets very low with petechiae / bruising / bleeding
- neutropenia with fever
- marked leukocytosis with severe illness signs
- hematuria with pain / clots / edema / high BP
- jaundice, dark urine, pale stool, severe abdominal pain
- child with lethargy, dehydration, poor intake, persistent vomiting

## Fast outpatient follow-up
- persistent mild anemia
- repeated leukopenia / thrombocytopenia
- persistent protein or blood in urine
- repeated elevated liver enzymes
- repeated high ESR without clear reason

- "По этому анализу видно направление, но не окончательный диагноз."

# 8. READY-MADE EXPLANATION BLOCKS

## CBC anemia block
По анализу есть признаки, которые могут соответствовать анемии или дефицитному состоянию. Чтобы понять тип анемии, важно смотреть не только на гемоглобин, но и на MCV, MCH, RDW, ретикулоциты и ферритин.

## Infection block
По лейкоцитарной формуле есть сдвиг, который может соответствовать инфекционному или воспалительному процессу. Для уточнения нужны жалобы, температура, длительность симптомов и иногда CRP.

## Allergy / parasite block
Повышение эозинофилов чаще бывает при аллергическом фоне или паразитарной инвазии, но без симптомов и дополнительного контекста этот показатель не даёт окончательного ответа.

## Urine block
Изменения в общем анализе мочи важно оценивать с учётом симптомов и качества сбора. Однократное отклонение ещё не всегда означает болезнь почек или мочевыводящих путей.

## Liver block
Небольшие отклонения печёночных ферментов требуют оценки в динамике и сопоставления с лекарствами, алкоголем, физической нагрузкой, весом, жалобами и УЗИ-контекстом при необходимости.
