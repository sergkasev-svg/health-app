STEP 3 - METABOLIC INTERPRETATION

Interpret via pathway logic:

IF metabolite elevated

-> identify required vitamin cofactor
-> identify possible mitochondrial dysfunction
-> identify toxic/metabolic stressor

STEP 4 - DIFFERENTIAL HYPOTHESIS
